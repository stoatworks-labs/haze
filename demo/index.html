Photosensitivity note

One setting on this page flashes: Pattern Audience. Every other step it swings every head to look straight into the camera, so the lenses blaze and the picture flares and falls back. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

The page opens on the plugin's own settings, the Fan pattern, which does not flash, and no preset uses Audience. At 120 BPM, Audience on a Step On of Beat flashes past three times a second in part of the picture, which is over the limit broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts on a large flash; at 4 a Beat it is past three a second over the whole picture. Every other pattern, at every step rate, measured no flashes. A link that opens on Audience opens paused. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
